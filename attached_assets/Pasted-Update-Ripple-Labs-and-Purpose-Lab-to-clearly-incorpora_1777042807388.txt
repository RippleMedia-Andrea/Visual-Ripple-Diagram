Update Ripple Labs and Purpose Lab to clearly incorporate The Ripple Method as the core methodology behind the app experience.

Purpose:
The Purpose Lab is built on The Ripple Method, a guided process that helps users uncover their purpose from their story, put language to it, and live it out in their current season of life.

Core belief:
Purpose is not something users create. It is something already within them that they uncover, name, and live.

Add The Ripple Method throughout the app as the organizing framework.

The Ripple Method stages are:

1. Reveal — Your Story
Users uncover meaningful stories, early experiences, moments of aliveness, challenges, and defining memories.

2. Identify — Your Patterns
The app helps users recognize patterns in their stories, including emotions, actions, values, strengths, impact, audience, motivations, and recurring themes.

3. Pinpoint — Your Purpose
Users turn those patterns into a clear purpose statement:
“I wake up every day to [action] so that [impact].”
Purpose should always end with impact on others.

4. Personalize — Your Season
Users apply their purpose to their current season of life, roles, responsibilities, and capacity.
Core teaching:
“Purpose remains constant, but its expression changes by season.”

5. Live — Your Ripple
Users create aligned action steps using Start / Stop / Continue.
They identify what needs to begin, what is misaligned, and what is already working.

6. Expand — Your Growth
Users integrate purpose into daily life through morning intention, evening reflection, and the ability to revisit Purpose Lab in future seasons.
