Add a new feature to the Purpose Lab called:

“Ripple Decision Maker”

Purpose:
This tool helps users make aligned decisions using their purpose statement, their current season of life, and their priorities.

The goal is to help users determine whether to say YES, NO, or NOT RIGHT NOW to opportunities, commitments, or decisions.

This should feel like a guided, reflective decision-making experience—not just a simple yes/no output.

--------------------------------------------------

PART 1: SEASON OF LIFE PROFILE (SETUP)

Create a section called:
“Your Current Season”

Purpose:
Help users clearly define the context of their life right now.

This should be a guided input experience with selectable options and optional custom inputs.

Include categories:

1. Life Stage
- Student (middle school, high school, college)
- Early career
- Mid-career
- Career transition
- Entrepreneur / business owner
- Stay-at-home parent
- Empty nester
- Retired
- Other (custom input)

2. Living Situation
- Living with parents
- Living alone
- Living with roommates
- Living with spouse/partner
- Other

3. Relationship Status
- Single
- Dating
- Married
- Divorced
- Widowed

4. Family Responsibilities
- No children
- Children (0–5)
- Children (6–12)
- Teenagers
- Adult children
- Caregiver (parent or other)
- Multiple selections allowed

5. Work / Time Commitments
- Full-time job
- Part-time job
- Business owner
- Student workload
- Multiple roles (allow selection)

6. Capacity Reflection (IMPORTANT)
Add a short prompt:
“How would you describe your current capacity?”
Options:
- Very full / overwhelmed
- Busy but manageable
- Balanced
- Spacious / open

Allow user to save this profile and edit later.

--------------------------------------------------

PART 2: PRIORITIES IDENTIFICATION

Create a section called:
“What Matters Most Right Now”

Purpose:
Help users identify and rank their current priorities.

Step 1: Select Priority Categories (multi-select)
- Faith / spiritual growth
- Personal growth
- Family
- Marriage / relationship
- Parenting
- Career / work
- Business
- Health
- Rest / recovery
- Community
- Finances
- Other (custom)

Step 2: Ranking
Have users select their top 3–5 priorities.

Step 3: Priority Clarification (IMPORTANT)
For each selected priority, ask:

“What does this look like in this season?”

Example:
If “Family” is selected:
→ “Being present in the evenings”
→ “Spending time with kids”

If “Work” is selected:
→ “Growing my business”
→ “Stability in income”

Allow short text input for each.

Save this data.

--------------------------------------------------

PART 3: DECISION INPUT

Create a section called:
“Make a Decision”

User inputs:
- “What decision are you trying to make?”
(text field)

- “What are you considering saying yes to?”
(text field)

Optional:
- urgency level (low / medium / high)

--------------------------------------------------

PART 4: DECISION ENGINE (AI RESPONSE)

Use the following inputs:
- user’s purpose statement
- season of life profile
- priorities
- decision input

Claude/AI should respond with:

1. ALIGNMENT REFLECTION
“Here’s how this aligns with your purpose…”

2. SEASON CHECK
“Considering your current season…”

3. PRIORITY CHECK
“This aligns / conflicts with your current priorities…”

4. INSIGHT SUMMARY
“What I’m noticing…”

5. GUIDED OUTCOME (NOT FORCEFUL)
Suggest one of:
- This is aligned → consider saying YES
- This may not be aligned → consider saying NO
- This may be right later → NOT RIGHT NOW

6. FINAL REFLECTION QUESTION
“Does this move you closer to the life you want to live, or further from it?”

--------------------------------------------------

TONE REQUIREMENTS:

- Calm
- Reflective
- Supportive
- Non-judgmental
- Do NOT tell the user what to do
- Guide them to clarity

--------------------------------------------------

UI NOTES:

- Clean, step-by-step flow
- Save user inputs for reuse
- Allow editing of season + priorities
- Show purpose statement at top of decision screen
- Use cards for clarity

--------------------------------------------------

IMPORTANT:

This tool should reinforce:
- identity
- purpose
- alignment

It should NOT feel like:
- a calculator
- a rigid system

It should feel like:
a thoughtful guide helping them make aligned decisions.
