Add three public pages to the website, styled like the rest of the site, readable on a phone, and linked in the footer (replace the current href="#" Privacy link in home.tsx):

/privacy: Privacy Policy (text below)
/terms: Terms of Use (text below)
/support: "Need help?" page with the support email, a short FAQ (How do I reset my password? How do I delete my account? Is my journey private? On Create Account (web and phone) add a required checkbox before the button: "I understand my answers are processed by a third-party AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to guide my Purpose Lab journey, as described in the Privacy Policy." with "Privacy Policy" linked. Also add "By creating an account you agree to the Terms of Use." with a link. Save ai_consent_at (timestamp) on the user record. Existing users who haven't consented see the same consent once, on their next visit to the journey, before continuing.

Part D — Password reset

Add "Forgot password?" on Sign In (web and phone) using Better Auth's password reset (sendResetPassword) with Resend for email (use Replit's Resend integration if available, otherwise the resend package with a RESEND_API_KEY secret and a RESET_EMAIL_FROM secret). The reset link opens a web page /reset-password where the user sets a new password; after success, show "Your password has been updated. You can sign in on the website or the app." If the Resend secrets are not set, hide the "Forgot password?" link instead of erroring. Email text: Subject: "Reset your Purpose Lab password" Body: "We received a request to reset your password. Tap the link below to choose a new one. If you didn't ask for this, you can ignore this email." + link.

Part E — Protect user edits

In ripple/extract.ts, if saved discoveries for a stage already exist and there are no new messages in that stage since they were last updated, return the saved discoveries instead of running extraction again (so going back and pressing Continue doesn't overwrite the user's edits). If there ARE new messages, run extraction as now.
