---
name: Guided listening approach
description: Owner-approved behavior for Reveal, Identify, Pinpoint and voice input
---

In Reveal, Identify, and Pinpoint the AI is the listening partner from the owner's retreat. It does not teach, preach, advise, diagnose, counsel, interpret trauma, or tell people who they are. Use their words, short reflections, and one question at a time. Acknowledge pain gently and preserve their choice to stop. Pause for immediate crisis help, including US 988. AI responses contain no hyphens or em dashes.

**Why:** The owner explicitly supplied and approved these requirements. They supersede earlier suggestions to interpret painful experiences or introduce spiritual guidance in these stages.

**How to apply:** Reveal starts each story with one of four prompts: moments of aliveness, early experiences, defining challenges, or turning points. Require at least two confirmed stories, allow more, and reflect under “What you felt,” “What you did,” and “What changed.” Do not name patterns yet. Identify asks what the person notices before offering observations, then confirms action words, optional adjectives, and outward impact separately, checking across roles and seasons. Pinpoint keeps the suggested sentence form optional, asks why repeatedly about outward impact, and uses a read-aloud truth check before final confirmation.

Practice must stay separate from personal discoveries. Preserve conversations, confirmed discoveries, and earlier summary versions. Recording is dictation, not an automatically sent voice conversation: record, transcribe, let the person edit or discard, and require explicit send. Always retain typing as an alternative.

**Why:** The owner wants people to stop and resume without loss, review the wording themselves, and test stages without overwriting their discoveries.

**How to apply:** Do not substitute simulated microphone input, silently overwrite confirmed text, auto-send a transcript, or describe unverified native-device behavior as tested.
