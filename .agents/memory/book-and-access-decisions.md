The product follows the book *Made for More*. The approved sequence is Reveal · Your Story, Identify · Your Patterns, Pinpoint · Your Purpose, Picture · Your Vision, Live · Your Season, Expand · Your Ripple.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved this terminology and wants the site and phone app aligned with the book.

**How to apply:** Treat action and outward impact equally. The suggested purpose statement form is optional. Picture explores vision and borrowed expectations; season work belongs in Live. Preserve earlier season reflections rather than treating them as vision answers. Expand's exercise is retained for the owner's review.

Course-buyer recognition must not introduce a payment gate for other users. Keep current access unchanged, and do not change pricing or publish without approval.

**Why:** The app has no actual checkout, and the user explicitly chose to preserve other users' existing access.

**How to apply:** Record verified course buyer entitlements server-side and support manual owner grants. The course is not built yet, so require an explicit qualifying course or offer before accepting automated grants.

Keep the exact tagline “What's within you creates a ripple.” and retain The Ripple Method™ until the user decides otherwise. Use warm conversational copy, “fulfilled life,” positive framing, and no em dashes.

**Why:** These are explicit brand and book copy instructions.

**How to apply:** Apply consistently across website, phone app, graphics, and film. Preserve user-mandated exact wording even when it contains negative wording.

The website rebrand uses navy `#0B1F4B` and gold `#C9A84C`, not the earlier teal palette. Preserve the home-page “Guided by Andrea John” card with Andrea’s actual photo and “Hi, I'm Andrea. Welcome in.”

**Why:** The user corrected repeated claims that the rebrand was already on Main when the site still used teal. These exact colors and the portrait card identify the intended design.

**How to apply:** Verify theme tokens and rendered headers, subtitles, buttons, and stage cards on Main. Matching branch contents or the presence of a dark color and gold accent does not prove the intended rebrand is present. Never substitute an invented portrait.
